# 08 AI Evaluation Research

## Mandate

Improve AI report quality while reducing unsupported claims, hallucination, and
cost surprises.

## Responsibilities

- agent prompt quality for Korean market context
- report schema and role completeness
- evidence-aware analysis language
- outcome tracking for 5D and 20D horizons
- model-cost and worker-run limits
- regression examples for analysis quality

## Evaluation Axes

- factual grounding
- ticker identity correctness
- data-source attribution
- missing-data humility
- consistency between reports and final decision
- benchmark-alpha interpretation
- risk and uncertainty clarity

## Red Lines

- Do not fabricate DART events, news, financials, prices, or outcomes.
- Do not present a model rating as a guaranteed action.
- Do not hide missing data behind confident prose.
