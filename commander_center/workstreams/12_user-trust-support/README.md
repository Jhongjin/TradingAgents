# 12 User Trust Support

## Mandate

Turn user confusion, support feedback, and trust objections into product and
content improvements.

## Responsibilities

- collect repeated confusion points from users
- write user-facing help copy and incident notices
- make auth, portfolio, and analysis queue states understandable
- identify places where Korean investors may over-trust AI output
- feed support patterns back into Product Planning and Compliance Policy

## Trust Signals To Maintain

- what data was used
- when it was generated
- what failed or is missing
- whether values are public market data, AI interpretation, or user-entered
  estimates
- what the system deliberately does not do
