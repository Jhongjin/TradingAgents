# 11 Compliance Policy

## Mandate

Keep the public service inside a research and information boundary. This
workstream does not replace legal counsel, but it owns the internal policy gate
for claims, disclaimers, and broker-integration risk.

## Responsibilities

- Korean and English disclaimer standards
- no-guaranteed-profit and no-personalized-advice copy review
- tax/legal caveat review
- broker read-only integration approval checklist
- quarterly review of market-hours, price-limit, tax, and fee assumptions
- public methodology page policy language

## Red Lines

- No suitability language for a specific investor.
- No guaranteed return or "AI knows the answer" framing.
- No legal or tax advice.
- No public claim that user-entered portfolio data is broker-verified.
- No broker execution UX without a separate legal, security, product, and risk
  review.

## Default Disclaimer Themes

- Informational research only.
- Not investment advice.
- No profit guarantee.
- Not legal or tax advice.
- No live trading or broker order placement.
- Market data and AI reports can be stale, incomplete, or wrong.
