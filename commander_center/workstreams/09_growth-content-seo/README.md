# 09 Growth Content SEO

## Mandate

Help the public site earn attention and ad revenue without sacrificing trust.

## Responsibilities

- public analysis feed editorial quality
- SEO titles, descriptions, sitemap, and robots
- AdSense readiness and `ads.txt`
- methodology, disclaimer, and trust copy
- content layout for high-intent Korean stock pages

## Content Rules

- Say what data was used.
- Say when analysis was generated.
- Say what the AI cannot know.
- Avoid guaranteed-return language.
- Keep user education concrete and short.

## Launch Needs

- public domain crawlable
- `TRADINGAGENTS_SITE_BASE_URL` matches canonical domain
- `ads.txt` configured after AdSense approval
- stock pages include stable canonical URLs
