# 01 Product Planning

## Mandate

Define what the service should do for Korean investors and keep the roadmap
focused on public AI analysis, member research workflows, and transparent risk
reporting.

## Core Questions

- What user problem does this feature solve?
- Is it public analysis, member workflow, or operator tooling?
- Does it improve trust, accuracy, retention, or monetization?
- What should be deferred until after launch?

## Current Focus

- member signup/login flow clarity
- manual portfolio trades, average cost, PnL, target, and stop
- watchlist workflow
- public analysis request queue
- methodology and trust pages

## Red Lines

- No personalized investment recommendation flow.
- No broker order UX.
- No product language implying guaranteed returns.
